Census

The Population and Housing Census provides the country's most comprehensive demographic profile. This section summarizes recent changes in the migrant population, its territorial distribution, age structure, and main countries of birth.

What this source shows

The Census supports analysis of regional and municipal distribution, sex and age structure, educational attainment, and selected household dimensions comparable between 2017 and 2024.

Methodological note

The figures distinguish indicators comparable across censuses from visualizations specific to 2017 or 2024.

Reuse and source

The data come from INE and were downloaded from its official website. The code and tables associated with these figures are available in the repository.
