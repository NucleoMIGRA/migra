CASEN

The National Socioeconomic Characterization Survey (CASEN) provides evidence on the living conditions of Chile's resident population. DataMigra presents indicators on income, poverty, housing, education, health, employment, and demographic characteristics for migrant and Chilean populations.

What this source shows

CASEN provides evidence on population size, composition, territorial distribution, educational pathways, and labor and socioeconomic outcomes among migrant and Chilean populations.

Methodological note

The figures combine indicators that are comparable over time with visualizations specific to the latest available survey round.

Reuse and source

The data come from the CASEN survey and were downloaded from its official website. The code and tables associated with these figures are available in the repository.
